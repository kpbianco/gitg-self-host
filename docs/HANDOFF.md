## Protected decisions

- Completion is not mastery, and human dignity or worth is never scored.
- Stable IDs and manifest-listed canonical files are authoritative.
- Historical legacy and typed evidence replay remains exact.
- Unknown, N/A, deferred, missing, contradictory, adverse, stale, invalid, and
  unconsented states remain explicit and fail closed or are withheld.
- Recommendation target subsets never replace the full canonical parent
  competency allocation used for scoring.
- Database activation drift, action drift, mapping drift, and lever-total drift
  fail closed.
- Published baselines, raw self-report, orientations, and archetypes remain
  immutable.
- Pending content and specialist audits remain visible. All-active software
  operation is not clinical, psychometric, cultural, accessibility, mastery,
  participant, longitudinal, or intervention-effectiveness validation.
- Release, deployment, participant exposure, and broader production claims
  require authorization separate from Decision 052.

# M6J-05 emotional maturity practice reader

This compact reader makes the domain 07 interventions easy to review without
mistaking a summary for the canonical content. The complete setup, instructions,
checks, adaptations and worked outcomes are in
`docs/authoring/exercises/07.yaml`; the compiler places those exact fields into
the manifest-listed runtime packages.

## 07.01 — Name one emotional episode precisely enough to choose a response

- Default: a friend changes a plan and an immediate sharp reply feels tempting.
- Work: record event/body/intensity/urge; compare at least three nearby emotion
  labels with evidence for and against; choose and later revise a matched response.
- Review: which distinction changed the response, and what remains uncertain?
- Boundary: no diagnosis, mind-reading or required access to internal sensation.

## 07.02 — Build and test an early body-signal cue for one ordinary stressor

- Default: opening a crowded inbox, with a neutral six-area comparison scan.
- Work: record the baseline; catch the earliest change on two natural occasions;
  pair a reliable signal with one safe response and evaluate its timing.
- Review: which cue was early enough to use, and when did the response fail?
- Boundary: no induced activation, forced breath focus or dismissal of symptoms.

## 07.03 — Install a brief pause before one consequential reaction

- Default: a frustrating but non-urgent message and a 60–120 second pause card.
- Work: name the reaction and cost; rehearse cue, pause, holding statement and
  return twice; use the sequence naturally and verify the issue was revisited.
- Review: did the pause improve the eventual action and preserve the issue?
- Boundary: no silent punishment, indefinite avoidance or delay of safety action.

## 07.04 — Test one low-stakes fear prediction through graduated approach

- Default: write, rehearse and then ask one routine meeting question.
- Work: bound the fear and select an objectively safe step rated 4/10 or below;
  attempt that same step twice; revise the prediction and next step from outcomes.
- Review: what did repeated attempts show, and what is the safest next step?
- Boundary: this is not clinical exposure and does not remove necessary supports.

## 07.05 — Turn one manageable anger episode into a proportionate repair

- Default: a borrowed household item repeatedly returned to the wrong place.
- Work: separate event, impact, value/boundary and ego amplification; make a
  consented direct request; review response, delivery repair and follow-through.
- Review: what information did anger contain, and was the response proportionate?
- Boundary: no intimidation, punishment or unsafe confrontation.

## 07.06 — Carry one bounded loss through remembrance, support and changed action

- Default: the end of a hobby group, opportunity or familiar place.
- Work: name what was lost, remains and changed; complete remembrance and support
  actions; carry one responsibility into ordinary life under the new conditions.
- Review: what deserves remembrance, and what now has to change?
- Boundary: no grief timetable, forced closure, gratitude or positive meaning.

## 07.07 — Convert global shame into specific responsibility and repair

- Default: a forgotten commitment that created extra work for a teammate.
- Work: replace the worth verdict with an accountable event record; separate
  repair, consequence and prevention; complete appropriate renewed conduct.
- Review: which responsibility became clearer, and what repair remains owed?
- Boundary: no compelled confession, demanded forgiveness or legal conclusion.

## 07.08 — Translate one comparison reaction into information and chosen action

- Default: a peer receives recognition the participant wanted.
- Work: distinguish admiration, need, scarcity, fear and controlling urge; test
  the assumptions; choose learning, gratitude, a boundary, a goal or disengagement.
- Review: what can be pursued cleanly, and which urge should not be obeyed?
- Boundary: no monitoring, possession, entitlement or sabotage.

## 07.09 — Express one difficult feeling without leaking it through control

- Default: tell a trusted friend that a changed recurring plan felt excluding.
- Work: prepare observation, feeling, need and request; ask consent and deliver it
  accessibly; review the listener's response, clarity and any delivery repair.
- Review: what was honest and proportionate, and what remained the listener's choice?
- Boundary: no required disclosure to unsafe or unwilling people.

## 07.10 — Pair self-compassion with repair after one ordinary failure

- Default: a missed self-imposed study session.
- Work: distinguish punishment, indulgence and accountable care; assign care,
  repair and consequence; complete a smaller renewed action and compare results.
- Review: which care made responsibility actionable, and what consequence remains?
- Boundary: care does not erase harm and accountability does not justify deprivation.

## 07.11 — Hold one hard fact beside a present good and a plausible future

- Default: a project delay, one enough-for-now stopping point and a fallback plan.
- Work: separate facts from predictions; receive one present good without using it
  to cancel hardship; complete one plausible future action with a contingency.
- Review: what stayed hard, available and plausible when all three were visible?
- Boundary: no compulsory optimism, gratitude, joy or abandonment of ambition.

## 07.12 — Run one evidence-based restart after a manageable setback

- Default: a household task not finished before guests arrive.
- Work: debrief facts, responsibility, outside conditions, recovery and support;
  change one controllable variable; attempt and compare a bounded restart.
- Review: which variable earned another test, and what remains unresolved?
- Boundary: no rapid-recovery demand, toughness metric or lesson forced from harm.

## 07.13 — Build and rehearse a location-specific mental-health help route

- Default: a private four-level card covering ordinary, routine professional,
  urgent specialized and emergency help in the participant's actual location.
- Work: define observable thresholds; verify reachable services and access needs;
  rehearse a concise handoff and use the real route now when a threshold is present.
- Review: could the correct help be reached under stress, and what is unverified?
- Boundary: no diagnosis, generic hotline assumption or worksheet before emergency action.

## 07.14 — Compare two regulation strategies across two ordinary contexts

- Default: desk deadline tension and irritation during a household transition.
- Work: define two contexts, two strategies and a behavioral marker; record four
  natural uses; build a conditional menu with ineffective-result and escalation rules.
- Review: what worked where, and when is self-guided regulation no longer enough?
- Boundary: no induced distress, treatment change or universal-effectiveness claim.

# M6J-06: attention, desire, temperance and discipline

Ten individually written exercises compile into the existing domain 08
runtime packages. The eleventh competency, 08.02, has a separately validated
source-only guide to its frozen presence experiment. It remains
`rewrite_pending` in runtime coverage: **115 authored, 268 pending**.
The source-only guide is not a replacement evidence contract.

| Canonical competency | Concrete scope-to-action relationship | Evidence and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 08.01 Attention audit | A1 samples devices, entertainment, work, relationships, repetitive worry and deliberate practice at three actual transitions. A2 selects a recurring cue and installs one environmental change. A3 compares an actual use with the usual setting. | A local attention leak and tested redesign; chosen leisure is not automatically waste, and unsampled life remains unknown. |
| 08.02 Mindfulness and present attention | The guide expands the existing usual/changed/repeat windows: return to a task or accessible environmental detail, distinguish past/future/stimulation drift, compare one safe condition and repeat within seven days. Body and interpersonal contexts are optional and bounded. | Existing 15-minute comparable windows, support retention and original evidence markers remain intact. No runtime rewrite or new scoring criteria are projected from the guide. |
| 08.03 Impulse control | A1 considers speech, spending, eating, sex, anger, quitting and novelty while selecting one ordinary reversible choice point. A2 performs a delay for an endorsed reason. A3 repeats and revises a poor fit. | Two actual optional choice points, not a requirement to enact every impulse category or override necessities and consent. |
| 08.04 Desire, craving and lust | A1 compares the desire's promise with neglected facts. A2 restores the broader value of people or goods and explicitly rejects entitlement. A3 makes a choice while allowing wanting to remain. | A nonsexual default permits an actual choice without buying or provoking craving. Relational and sexual desire are considered through agency and boundaries, not compulsory disclosure or enactment. |
| 08.05 Tolerance of discomfort | A1 distinguishes boredom, uncertainty, frustration, embarrassment, fatigue, craving and pain, selecting only a small appropriate task. A2 attempts it with ordinary discomfort allowed. A3 checks an actionable reminder and reviews continued action or a legitimate stop. | The supplied item/time/place task needs no invented rubric. No pain exposure, exhaustion target or claim that an easy task tests every discomfort. |
| 08.06 Action independent of motivation | Setup defines a reversible two-to-five-minute start and two suitable low-motivation windows. A1 attempts the usual-condition start. A2 adds exactly one cue for a separate start. A3 compares zero, one or two starts and context differences. | The original Boolean, duration, count, supporting and adverse evidence rules are retained exactly. Missing low-motivation windows stay inconclusive; output and capacity are not scored. |
| 08.07 Habit and environment design | A1 identifies cue, default, friction, checklist, layout and optional accountability. A2 installs one arrangement. A3 uses it at two recurring opportunities and revises a failed link. | An installed notebook/departure arrangement can carry part of the burden. No permanent-habit claim follows two uses, and helper support stays explicit. |
| 08.08 Consistency and minimum standards | A1 defines a substantive minimum and higher normal standard with explicit omissions. A2 performs both using supplied learning content. A3 selects a fitting level at a natural opportunity and examines longer-term adequacy. | A reduced accurate sentence differs from a fuller explanation and example. A trial is not difficult-day continuity; minimum performance is not full performance. |
| 08.09 Voluntary restraint and hardship | A1 compares possible forms and chooses safe optional simplification with a purpose, end and stop rule. A2 performs it without seeking suffering. A3 ends and assesses choice, gratitude, humility and rigidity. | Simplicity, silence and optional abstention are legitimate alternatives. Fasting, difficult training and physical deprivation are not prescribed as generic substitutes; no escalation follows an easy trial. |
| 08.10 Completion and perseverance | A1 limits active commitments and defines done. A2 addresses a specific plateau instead of switching automatically. A3 checks a finished invitation or explicitly ends a real goal with reasons and affected-person consequences. | A supplied four-part invitation gives a complete default. Draft completion, a real event and an uncommunicated cancellation remain different outcomes. |
| 08.11 Discipline without domination or purity | A1 compares purpose with health, duty and meaning costs, self-attack, compulsion and superiority. A2 writes a precise legitimate exception. A3 uses or rehearses it and reviews whether both values were served. | The optional reading/conversation case tests flexible structure without changing medical, safety or shared obligations. Neither punishment nor blanket permissiveness is the goal. |

Each entry contains a goal, scope, supplied default, materials, burden, three
distinct actions with four observable checks each, adaptation, meaningful
review and supportive/mixed/contradictory/inconclusive examples. The ten
runtime rewrites provide 30 instructions, 120 checks and 40 examples; the
guide adds three explanatory actions, 12 reading-aid checks and four examples.

The loader validates all declared guides and permits exclusions only for the
five known frozen legacy IDs. Missing, duplicate, foreign or nonlegacy guide
exclusions fail. Guide text never enters `apply_exercise`; that function still
rejects legacy projection. Runtime coverage retains the guide's pending row
and lists the guide separately rather than representing it as finished.

All other 373 practice packages, including the frozen presence protocol, must
remain byte-identical. Stable IDs, action counts, parent mappings, activation,
assessment, ranking, scoring and explicit human closeout are unchanged.
This is original educational content and software verification, not human
semantic, accessibility, clinical, cultural or empirical acceptance.

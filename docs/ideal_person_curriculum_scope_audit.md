## Coverage logic

A domain remained separate when it met all four tests:

1. It develops a distinct object or sphere of human life.
2. It has characteristic failures that cannot be reduced to another domain.
3. It requires distinct practices or knowledge.
4. Progress can be evidenced differently from neighboring domains.

The taxonomy was checked against the genres present in the original 876-item source corpus, including positivity and resilience; friendship and social connection; romance, sex, family, and caregiving; finance and business; virtue and honor; religion and providence; learning and teaching; rhetoric and public speaking; physical training and nutrition; practical household skills; survival and water competence; philosophy and truth; time, goals, work, and legacy; art, travel, nature, and contentment.

It was also checked for commonly underrepresented life genres: mental-health help-seeking, consent and bodily autonomy, parenting and dependent care, civic literacy, cybersecurity and fraud, institutional power, ecological stewardship, life transitions, aging, succession, and repair after harm.

## Deliberate source transformations

The following source patterns were not preserved literally:

- Gender stereotypes became general interpersonal, relational, bodily, and family competencies.
- Seduction or manipulation tactics became respectful courtship, consent, communication, and partner selection.
- Absolute self-sacrifice became sustainable service, reciprocal care, and proportionate responsibility.
- Class-coded status habits became dignity, etiquette, competence, and freedom from status consumption.
- Claims against appropriate medicine became health literacy and informed collaboration with clinicians.
- Religious absolutes became competencies that can be instantiated within a committed tradition or approached through pluralistic or secular inquiry.
- Reputation management was retained only where it reflects trustworthiness, not concealment or image control.
- Stoic composure was separated from emotional suppression and denial.

## Recommended next layer

The original concrete tasks should now be mapped beneath these master competencies as one or more of:

- concept lesson
- diagnostic reflection
- first practice
- repeated deliberate-practice exercise
- real-world challenge
- evidence submission
- review or reassessment

That mapping preserves the richness of the 876-item corpus while preventing the user-facing curriculum from becoming an undifferentiated list.

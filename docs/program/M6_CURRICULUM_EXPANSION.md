# M6 curriculum expansion program

## Charter

M6 turns the five-protocol vertical slice into a controlled, individually
authored developmental system covering all 383 canonical competencies.
Grounded Growth remains a pluralist capability and practice tool:

- human dignity, virtue, perfection, and moral worth are never scored;
- self-report initializes or supplements a hypothesis and never proves broad
  competence;
- not applicable, defer, and alternative pathways produce no deficit;
- disability, culture, resources, role, relationship, season, and worldview
  are design context rather than noise;
- completion, protocol performance, transfer, competency evidence, and lever
  state remain distinct;
- important factual, intervention, safety, and measurement claims require
  appropriate sources and stated limitations;
- false precision is rejected. A non-scored, shadow-only, corroborated,
  artifact, objective, or qualified-evidence disposition may be the honest
  policy.

This is a multi-PR program. It is not authorization to generate 378
noun-swapped or empty packages.

The old Notion-ranked-task source has 383 unique task, scope, and
evidence-of-progress values but one identical Journal Prompt on every row.
The M6 originality gate treats that as a known defect to prevent, not a
template to propagate.

The five runtime packages retain 15 unique action instructions. Their uniform
three-action shape, four ten-day durations, two duplicate legacy evidence-rule
payloads, and similar recommendation framing remain visible warnings. They are
compatibility facts, not patterns for future mass authoring.

## Program phases

### M6A — Canonical content foundation

- establish schemas, registries, risk and scoring taxonomies, activation
  ledger, research gaps, expert queue, generated coverage/originality reports,
  and a release manifest;
- project the five reviewed protocols without changing IDs, copy, behavior,
  availability, action rules, or score activation;
- retain the old readiness verifier and add an independent additive contract;
- add no ORM migration, new protocol, typed evidence execution, scoring
  mathematics, or UI.

### M6B — Competency evidence and scoring architecture

- resolve assessment baseline versus protocol evidence versus direct
  competency evidence versus lever state;
- design backward-compatible typed evidence and explicit unknown,
  contradiction, adverse, provenance, and withholding behavior;
- add pure domain implementations, accepted ADR updates, exact synthetic
  golden fixtures, property tests, replay, reversal, and no-baseline-mutation
  proofs;
- keep production scoring at the existing friendship-only boundary.

### M6C — Context-aware priority and Personal OS foundation

- add the minimum models and services for applicability, importance,
  readiness, urgency, opportunity/resources, current season and capacity,
  defer/not-now, mission, principles, anti-goals, and priority stack;
- keep personality as a framing or tie-break input only;
- add a concise Truth/Autopilot Audit and Personal OS experience;
- demonstrate useful alternative recommendations after “not now” with no
  deficit interpretation;
- preserve concise home and practice flows, with setup under five minutes
  where possible and ordinary check-ins under two minutes.

### Phase B — Representative vertical slices

Author a deliberately varied set of approximately 10–12 competencies before
scaling: low, moderate, and high-risk classes; behavioral, conceptual,
artifact, relational, contemplative, health, and qualified-review families;
cross-context, role-conditional, pathway-dependent, and elective content; and
several domains and lever families. Keep score mutation shadow-only unless one
protocol independently clears its activation gate.

The purpose is to repair schema, evidence, safety, and UX defects before they
are multiplied. Do not proceed to mass authoring while material defects remain.

### Phase C — Full curriculum authoring

Process the curriculum domain by domain. Prefer 8–15 individually authored
packages per PR and split large domains into coherent sub-batches. Each batch
updates canonical content, claim-level sources, coverage and risk reports,
fixtures, and UI coverage together. Stop for owner review after every PR.

### Phase D — Whole-library scoring calibration

Prove that every protocol has an explicit scoring disposition, direction-
complete family/risk fixtures, and state/replay invariants. Audit evidence mass
and task-to-lever scaling without presenting software calibration as
psychometric validation.

### Phase E — Controlled score activation

Activate only reviewed cohorts with stable mappings, evidence rules, source
and risk review, fixtures, replay tests, explanatory UI copy, and explicit
approval. Never create a global “activate all” switch.

### Phase F — Full-product integration and validation

Integrate Personal OS context with recommendations and weekly execution,
complete concise explorer/profile flows, add non-coercive upcoming-action
support, prove export/deletion/retention and backup/restore/upgrade behavior,
and conduct diverse multi-cycle accessibility, cultural-bias, safety, burden,
abandonment, and recommendation-fit review. Maintain modest claims.

## Per-package release gate

A package is not a release candidate until:

1. Stable protocol, action, parent competency, domain, family, risk, policy,
   source, and activation references validate.
2. Purpose, applicability, N/A handling, intervention, actions, burden,
   adaptations, privacy, stop, and escalation logic are specific.
3. Evidence measures observable performance or outcomes rather than worth,
   sentiment, verbosity, or mere completion.
4. Independence, context, repetition, recency, contradiction, adverse
   outcomes, minimum evidence, and withholding are explicit.
5. Completion, transfer, and mastery are separated.
6. Pre/during/post reflection and supportive, mixed, contradictory, and
   inconclusive examples are individualized.
7. Important claims have suitable sources, classifications, and limitations.
8. Risk-class review and required specialist review are recorded.
9. Exact and near-duplicate reports have been reviewed; shared language is
   confined to approved structural or safety blocks.
10. Deterministic import, replay, report, unit/integration, browser, image, and
    deployment checks pass where applicable.

Text similarity can identify review candidates. It cannot prove semantic
operationalization; that remains a recorded human-review decision.

## Batch operating rule

For every later batch:

1. start from current reviewed `main`;
2. select only the next approved ledger rows;
3. audit the exact competency and existing runtime boundary;
4. research and inspect sources rather than relying on snippets;
5. state the batch exclusions and acceptance criteria;
6. update packages, registries, reports, tests, and UI fixtures together;
7. run all relevant deterministic, replay, browser, image, and Compose checks;
8. report failures, skips, and unverified criteria plainly;
9. open a focused draft PR and stop for owner approval;
10. never merge the agent's own PR.

## Explicit non-goals for M6A

- no 378 protocol stubs or generated pseudo-content;
- no new runtime protocol or action;
- no ORM or UI expansion;
- no new evidence type or changed evidence/scoring/ranking mathematics;
- no second score-active protocol;
- no changed assessment v1.1 or existing hash semantics;
- no claim that the five migrated packages are externally validated,
  source-complete, psychometric, universal, or proof of mastery.

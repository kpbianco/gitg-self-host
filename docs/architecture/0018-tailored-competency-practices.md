# ADR 0018: Tailored, self-contained competency practices

Status: Owner-directed implementation, 2026-09-05.

The owner requires every competency to have a rich practice that represents
its canonical goal, can be followed without inventing an intervention, and
can be adapted by people with different circumstances. Catalog coverage alone
does not meet this requirement. M6J replaces the generated exercise content
and reviews the nine earlier packages against the same standard.

Delivery follows the existing multi-PR M6 program. M6J-01 rewrites the 11
meaning/worldview, 15 ethics/integrity and 16 domestic practices, including the
earlier home-upkeep package. It leaves 341 explicitly pending. Completing or merging
this cohort does not close the catalog-wide requirement. Subsequent cohorts
must supply equally concrete content, not merely increase a counter.

Each practice must supply a concrete outcome, prerequisites and materials,
an immediately usable default example, ordered instructions, observable proof
for each action, a meaningful review, and an accessible alternative. Abstract
requests to create a rubric, pick an unspecified component, or invent an
exercise are insufficient. A simulated alternative must say what it exercises
and what remains untested. A single practice must state its bounded relation
to the whole competency rather than claim comprehensive mastery.

Authored content is indexed by exact competency IDs. Shared code may serialize
content and enforce schemas; it must not select an exercise by keyword,
domain, personality, or a noun-substitution template. Runtime continues to
consume only the canonical practice packages. Authoring inputs are compiler
inputs, never a second runtime catalog. Missing content fails closed.

The existing 383 protocol identities, parent mappings, 1,151 action identities,
activation records, assessment instrument, ranking formula, action weights,
human-closeout trigger, partial/full credit and repeat aggregation stay fixed.
Updated action evidence remains versioned in immutable event snapshots; old
snapshots are replayed under their own rules. Import must refuse a changed
practice while an active or paused sprint depends on its prior instructions.
The operator can finish or explicitly stop that sprint before retrying import.

The home-upkeep package also retains its original typed evidence rules and
golden fixture expectations. Its richer instructions explain the same audit,
redesign, installation and two-opportunity review. Future changes to other
pre-frontier packages need the same explicit compatibility disposition.

Professional, empirical, safety and intervention claims need inspected sources
with a precise support boundary. Product-authored exercises must not claim
that a referenced source tested this exact exercise. Factual source support,
editorial completeness, software correctness, human review and empirical
validation remain separately recorded. No review is marked complete by a
generator, source link, coverage counter, or passing test.

Completion requires the existing minimum and meaningful-attempt rule. Deferral,
N/A, intentions and blank worksheets are not completed actions. The exercise
provides explicit observed/not-observed criteria and permits mixed or negative
results without pretending that completion proves skill, worth or mastery.

The acceptance review spans all 27 domains and every canonical scope. Generic
or role-dependent content is revised, not hidden behind a completion count.
The five legacy protocols retain their frozen runtime compatibility, but their
source-only guides and examples may be enriched where needed.

The owner reiterated this requirement on 2026-09-06 and redirected work away
from publication mechanics toward the content itself. Each continuation must
map the canonical scope and evidence-of-progress goal to its actual actions,
examples and review. Naming a facet without explaining or exercising it is
insufficient. When the canonical scope lists alternative religious forms,
life situations or roles, distinguish legitimate chosen pathways from missing
coverage; never require incompatible beliefs, invented distress or an absent
real-world opportunity merely to fill a checklist.

The standalone reading test is concrete: can an unfamiliar person explain
what the competency involves, obtain the stated materials, follow the supplied
default without inventing a rubric, recognize supportive and adverse outcomes,
adapt the exercise without losing its purpose, and identify the next review?
Rehearsal, source study, live participation and long-term competence must stay
distinguishable. M6J-03 records this mapping for every competency in domains
03 and 04 and supplies a readable projection of the complete new instructions.
The catalog-wide target remains all 383; 77 authored records do not resolve
the remaining 306 rewrites or any deferred human or empirical review.

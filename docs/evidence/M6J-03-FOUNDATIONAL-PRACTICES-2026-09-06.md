## Substantive review

The scope review checked each canonical facet against the actual tasks. It
identified and corrected two gaps before the final compilation:

- Metaphysical inquiry now supplies contrasting positions for all six named
  questions, while identifying the single question examined more deeply.
- End-of-life accompaniment now includes a usable listening response that
  acknowledges uncertainty and offers a choice instead of false reassurance.

Other explicit distinctions include the relation between wisdom texts,
historical setting and a living community; gratitude without unlimited debt;
community contribution rather than research alone; genuine disagreement in
pluralism; moral-injury literacy without diagnosis or compelled confession;
and hopeful action that preserves adverse evidence and revisable expectations.

This is an authoring review, not independent human semantic certification.

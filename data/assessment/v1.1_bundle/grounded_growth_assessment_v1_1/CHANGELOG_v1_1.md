# v1.0 to v1.1 Change Log

## Defects fixed

- Fixed the timer-reset defect that produced an invalid 40.8-second total in Pilot 001.
- Prevented an extreme speed flag from multiplying confidence down by 35%.
- Reduced false Counselor inflation caused by connection questions that mixed social detection with relational preference.
- Reduced false stewardship suppression caused by wording that sounded limited to interpersonal caretaking.
- Removed the `Architect` naming collision with INTJ.
- Corrected the results interface so a conservative posterior is not presented as though it were the user's direct answer.

## Deliberately not changed yet

- The 37-lever ontology.
- The task-to-lever mappings.
- The Beta evidence-update architecture.
- Broad-lever decomposition such as technical leadership versus people leadership.
- Psychometric item calibration.

Those belong to the next fine-tuning and pilot-data phase rather than this cursory bug-fix pass.

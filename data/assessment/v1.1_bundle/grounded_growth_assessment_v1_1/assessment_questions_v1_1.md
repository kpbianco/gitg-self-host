# Grounded Growth Initial Alignment and Capability Assessment v1.1

## What changed

- Fixed per-question timing so selecting an answer no longer resets the clock.
- Reduced the severity of timing and response-style penalties.
- Reworded orientation items to distinguish connection, stewardship, agency, exploration, and transcendence more clearly.
- Renamed The Architect archetype to The Systems Steward to avoid collision with the MBTI INTJ nickname.
- Results now display raw self-report, calibrated estimate, and confidence separately.
- Added clearer archetype confidence and interpretation language.

## Response anchors

**Orientation:** 1 = Least like me; 3 = Mixed/context-dependent; 5 = Most like me.

**Capability:** 1 = rarely demonstrated; 2 = demonstrated with support; 3 = reliable in familiar low-pressure situations; 4 = reliable across ordinary situations; 5 = reliable under pressure/novelty and adaptable or teachable.

Use **N/A** when you lack relevant experience.
